What Changed

Between the original and edited version, the house roof colour was changed from red to blue, the door was darkened to a deep red, and the tree was shifted slightly and coloured a deeper green. The sun was made smaller and moved to a new position in the sky, and the whole house (including chimney, windows, and door) was repositioned as a unit. The walkway was also changed from a straight path in the original to a curved path in the edited version.
