What Changed

Between the original and edited version, the house roof colour was changed from red to blue, the door was darkened to a deep red, and the tree was shifted slightly and coloured a deeper green. The sun was made smaller and moved to a new position in the sky, and the house group itself was repositioned. The walkway was also made noticeably thicker and redrawn with a stronger curve, giving it a more winding, natural path toward the house instead of a nearly straight line.
